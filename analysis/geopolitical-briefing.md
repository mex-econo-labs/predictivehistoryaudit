## 1. US-Iran Military Actions

### The Twelve-Day War (June 13-24, 2025)
- Israel launched surprise strikes on Iranian nuclear facilities, military bases, and infrastructure on **June 13, 2025**, using 200+ fighter jets dropping 330+ munitions on ~100 targets in five waves.
- Iran retaliated with **550+ ballistic missiles and 1,000+ suicide drones**, hitting civilian population centers, a hospital, and at least 12 military/energy/government sites in Israel.
- On **June 22, 2025**, the US entered the conflict with **Operation Midnight Hammer**: 7 B-2 Spirit stealth bombers from Whiteman AFB flew 18 hours eastward, deploying 14 GBU-57A/B Massive Ordnance Penetrator "bunker buster" bombs on three nuclear facilities -- Fordow, Natanz, and Isfahan. Tomahawk cruise missiles were also fired from a submarine.
- A ceasefire was mediated by the US and Qatar, taking effect **June 24, 2025**.
- Pentagon assessment (July 2025): Iran's nuclear program set back approximately 2 years.

### The 2026 Iran War (February 28 - Ongoing)
- By **February 14, 2026**, the US publicly began a military buildup described as the largest in the Middle East since the 2003 Iraq invasion.
- On **February 28, 2026**, the US and Israel launched nearly **900 strikes in 12 hours** targeting Iranian missiles, air defenses, military infrastructure, and leadership.
- The opening salvo **assassinated Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei** and several family members in Tehran. His daughter Boshra, her daughter, his son-in-law, and daughter-in-law were also killed.
- A missile struck a girls' school adjacent to a naval base in Minab, near Bandar Abbas, killing ~170 people.
- As of March 31, 2026: **Al Jazeera tracker**: **2,000+ killed**. **HRANA**: **3,461 killed** (1,551 civilians, 236 children) as of Mar 29. **Red Crescent**: **1,900+ killed, 20,000+ injured**. **Washington Post** (Mar 27): **~1,500 civilians killed**. **Hengaw**: **5,300 killed in 18 days** (511 civilians, 4,789 military). The **Iranian Red Crescent Society** reported **82,000+ civilian structures** damaged or destroyed (Mar 24). US has hit **two-thirds of Iran's missile and drone production facilities**. Iran's internet blackout has made independent casualty verification extremely difficult (France24, SCMP). US acknowledges war may extend past initial **4-6 week timeline** (Times of Israel, Mar 27). *Sources: Modern Diplomacy, HRANA, Al Jazeera, WashPost, Hengaw*
- Iran has responded with **500+ ballistic/naval missiles and ~2,000 drones** since Feb 28, striking US military presence and civilian infrastructure across **9 countries** including Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Oman, and the UAE.
- At least **22+ Israelis killed and 3,950+ wounded** in Iranian retaliatory attacks. Iranian missiles struck Dimona and Arad on March 21-22, wounding **180** in the most significant penetration of Israeli air defenses near nuclear infrastructure. On March 25, Iranian missiles struck **Tel Aviv** after a **David's Sling malfunction** allowed ballistic missiles through — 4+ casualties, buildings damaged. Israel experienced **seven waves of Iranian missiles** since midnight March 25.
- **15 US service members killed** (confirmed by casualty trackers as of Mar 31; includes 7 killed by enemy fire in Gulf, 6 in Iraq plane crash Mar 13, 1 died of wounds at Prince Sultan, 1 additional confirmed) and **300+ wounded** (up from ~200 as of Mar 22; 10-15 injured in Prince Sultan Air Base attack Mar 28).
- A US Senate **war powers vote failed**, allowing Trump to continue Iran strikes without new authorization.

- Struck **90+ military targets**: runway, naval base, air defenses, mine storage facilities, missile storage bunkers.

- **Oil infrastructure deliberately spared**. Trump warned he would "immediately reconsider" if Iran continued Hormuz blockade.
- **March 20**: Axios reported Trump considering **occupation or blockade** of Kharg Island, with three sources saying ground troops on the island are "under serious consideration."

His son Morteza and aide Alireza Bayat also killed. Separately, **Basij militia commander Gholamreza Soleimani** killed in another strike the same night.

### Iran Rejects Trump's 15-Point Peace Plan (March 25-26, 2026)
- **March 25-26**: Iran formally **rejected** Trump's 15-point ceasefire proposal, according to state-run Press TV citing an anonymous official. A diplomatic source told Al Jazeera that Tehran dismissed the plan as **"maximalist, unreasonable"** and "unworkable."
- Iran laid out **five counter-conditions** for ending the war:
  1. Halt to "aggression and assassinations" by the US and Israel
  2. Establishment of "mechanisms" to ensure the war doesn't resume
  3. Payment of war damages and reparations
  4. End to US and Israeli attacks against Hezbollah in Lebanon and pro-Iranian militias in Iraq
  5. "International recognition and guarantees" for Iran's authority over the Strait of Hormuz
- Foreign Minister **Abbas Araghchi** stated on state TV: **"We have not engaged in talks to end the war, and we do not plan on any negotiations."** He clarified that US messages through mediators "does not mean negotiations."
- **White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt** insisted negotiations remain "productive" despite the reported rejection. A US official said the administration had "yet to receive any messages from Iran rejecting the offer."
- Trump vowed to **"hit harder"** — escalatory rhetoric as the March 28 deadline approaches.
- Iran's fifth condition — **Hormuz sovereignty recognition** — is a non-starter for Washington and signals the fundamental incompatibility of the two sides' positions.
- *Sources: Al Jazeera, NPR, Time, Axios, Bloomberg, CNBC, Euronews, Daily Caller, Boston Globe*
